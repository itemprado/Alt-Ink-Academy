## 🏰 1. Renaissance Cross-Hatching Technique

* **The Agony of 90° Crosses:** Never cross hatching lines at 90° right angles (this creates ugly window-screen meshes).
* **The 60° Golden Cross:** Always cross secondary hatch lines at **45° to 60°**.
* **Hatching Intervals:** Keep spacing strictly parallel at **0.5 mm**. Use your 1205RL @ 6.8V like a Renaissance burin tool.

## 🦾 2. Forearm Torsion & Anatomical Body Flow

* **The Radius & Ulna Trap:** When a client rotates their wrist (pronation vs. supination), the radius bone crosses over the ulna, displacing forearm skin by up to 45°.
* **Golden Rule of Stencil Placement:** **ALWAYS apply the stencil with the client standing upright** in a relaxed, anatomical posture (palms facing the thighs).
* **Elbow Ring Expansion:** The olecranon (elbow tip) stretches skin by 40% when bent. Never place delicate faces or tight text directly over the elbow joint; design concentric negative-space rings or organic filigree.
