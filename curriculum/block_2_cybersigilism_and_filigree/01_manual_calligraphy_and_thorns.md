# Block 2: Cybersigilism, Dynamic Spikes & Gothic Filigree

Cybersigilism blends tribal aggressiveness with organic Gothic filigree, characterized by knife-sharp needle-tapered spikes and seamless interlocking arcs.

---

## ⚔️ 1. The Tapered Needle Whip Release (El Despegue)

Unlike anime fineline where line weight is uniform, cybersigilism spikes transition from a solid 1.2 mm base down to a hairline micro-point:
1. **Entrance (75°):** Enter the skin with needle hang at 1.8 mm, sinking to full dermal depth (1.5 mm).
2. **Body Acceleration:** As you accelerate the hand stroke, gradually lift the machine upwards.
3. **Takeoff (85° Whip):** Erect the machine toward 85° while flicking the wrist outward. The needle exits the skin at speed, leaving a needle-thin razor tip.

---

## 🔗 2. The Invisible Line Splice (Empalme Invisible)

For long sweeping chandelier filigree curves that exceed your comfortable 4 cm hand span:
1. **Feather the Exit:** Decelerate and lift the needle gradually over the last 3 mm of the line (feathering the stroke to 50% opacity).
2. **Rotate the Canvas:** Reposition the synthetic skin so the continuation pulls naturally toward your body.
3. **Re-enter in the Ramp:** Place the needle 2 mm inside the feathered zone with the motor running, match hand speed, and continue the line seamlessly.
