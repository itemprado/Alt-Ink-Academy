# Block 1: Machine Setup, Grip Wrapping & Anime Fineline

A great tattoo starts with ergonomic machine preparation. Operating a bare metal pen causes tendon strain, excessive grip pressure, and hand tremors after just 15 minutes.

---

## 🦾 1. The Pear-Shaped Grip Wrap (Grip Tape Wrapping)

Your Poseidon Pen has a polished aluminum body with a 25 mm diameter:
1. **Paper Towel Cushioning:** Fold two layers of kitchen paper towel around the grip section. This dampens 70% of motor vibration.
2. **Cohesive Wrap Build-Up:** Wrap with self-adhesive cohesive tape until reaching an ergonomic **35–40 mm diameter** in a teardrop or pear shape.
3. **Collar Clearance:** Leave **3 mm clearance** below the rotating click-adjustment mechanism so you can tune your needle hang freely during the session without removing the tape.

---

## 📐 2. The Golden 75° Drag Angle

* **Capillary Ink Flow:** Cartridge needles feed ink downward via gravity and capillary tension between the 5 bundled micro-needles.
* **Always pull toward your chest:** Maintain a **75° angle** inclined away from the direction of stroke.
* **Why not 90°?** Vertical alignment causes the needle to bounce against the skin surface, producing dotted lines and ink spatter.
* **Why not < 60°?** Severe angles push ink sideways into subcutaneous tissue, causing permanent bluish blowouts.

---

## 🎀 3. Anime Fineline: Marceline & Hello Kitty

* **Single-Pass Discipline:** Anime contours rely on unbroken, uniform lines without re-tracing.
* **Breathing Technique:** Inhale before starting the contour, exhale slowly as you pull the stroke. Never hold your breath tensely.
* **Tripod Skate:** Rest your pinky finger lightly on the silicone with a dab of petroleum jelly (*Pinky Skate*) to maintain constant depth.
