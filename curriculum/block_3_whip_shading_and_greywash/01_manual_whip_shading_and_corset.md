# Block 3: Whip Shading, Greywash Formulation & 3D Corset Lace

Transition from 2D linework to three-dimensional sculptural depth using your **1207RM (7 Curved Magnum)**.

---

## 🎨 1. The 45° Pendulum Whip Shading

* **Cartridge:** 1207RM (Curved Magnum). The arched needle configuration prevents the sharp outer corners from gouging the skin.
* **Voltage:** **5.8 V – 6.0 V** (Lower voltage slows down needle reciprocation, allowing distinct dot-peppering).
* **Angle:** **45° Skimming Arc**.
* **Movement:** Sweep the needle across the skin like a pendulum: dip in at the deepest shadow, skim the skin surface, and whip upward into light. Always stroke in one direction.

---

## 🧪 2. The 4-Cup Greywash Formulation

Prepare 4 standard #10 ink caps on your workstation:
* **Cup 1 (100% Black):** Pure dynamic lining black (for deep recesses, creases, and solid packing).
* **Cup 2 (50% Dark Greywash):** 15 drops black ink + fill with distilled water / witch hazel.
* **Cup 3 (25% Medium Greywash):** 6 drops black ink + fill with distilled water.
* **Cup 4 (10% Light Greywash):** 2 drops black ink + fill with distilled water (for delicate velvet skin tones).

---

## 🎀 3. 3D Corset Ribbon Depth Protocol

1. Tattoo ribbon contours with 1205RL @ 7.2V.
2. Clean skin thoroughly with Dab & Lift.
3. Use Cup 2 with 1207RM @ 5.8V to place deep shadows **immediately beneath each overlapping ribbon**. The contrast pushes the top ribbon forward in photorealistic 3D.
