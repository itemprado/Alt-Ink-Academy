# Block 5: Medieval Heraldry, Armorial Blazons & Dark Baroque Lace

Combine precision heraldic insignia with delicate gothic lingerie lace and high-contrast negative-space solar blackwork.

---

## 🛡️ 1. Symmetrical Heraldic Crest Discipline

* **Central Vertical Axis:** Heraldic crests require absolute symmetry. Draw a centerline on your stencil and tattoo central components (helmet visor, shield dividing line, ribbon center) first.
* **Knight Helmet Visor Slits:** Use `1205RL @ 7.2V` to pull crisp parallel slits. Leave a bright vertical stripe of untouched skin across the visor to simulate metallic steel reflection.
* **Acanthus Mantling Wings:** Treat flowing helmet plumes with the tapered whip technique, thickening the spine while feathering the curls.

---

## ☀️ 2. Negative-Space Solar Burst (The Sunburst Law)

* **Untouched Virgin Skin:** The core radiant sun must remain 100% white skin.
* **Vaseline Barrier:** Before packing the dark background, apply a thick protective bead of petroleum jelly directly over the sun center to repel stray ink.
* **Flare Rays:** Whip the 1205RL outward from the negative perimeter, feathering the ray tips into the dense surrounding blackwork.
