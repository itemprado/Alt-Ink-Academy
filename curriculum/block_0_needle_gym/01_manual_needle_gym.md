# Block 0: The Needle Gym — Foundations of Hand Speed & Depth

Welcome to the foundational training module of Alt-Ink Academy. Before attempting anime characters, cybersigilism spikes, or complex heraldry, you must mechanicalize your hand motion and calibrate your motor depth perception on synthetic silicone skin.

---

## ⚡ 1. The 15-Minute Daily Routine

Start every single tattoo practice session with Sheet 00:
1. **Parallel Lines (10 Passes):**
   * Needle: `1205RL` (5 Round Liner).
   * Voltage: **7.2 V** on Poseidon digital battery.
   * Needle Hang: **1.5 mm** extended while motor is running.
   * Hand Speed: Pull steadily at ~1 cm/second toward your body.
   * Objective: Achieve consistent line weight without wobble, stutter, or ink pooling.
2. **Continuous Sine Waves:**
   * Keep the 75° drag angle constant while gently steering from the elbow and shoulder, not by twisting your wrist.
3. **The 4 Gothic Micro-Motifs:**
   * **Gothic Bat:** Sharp wing tips with micro-flicks.
   * **4-Point Star:** Symmetrical diamond axes.
   * **Crescent Moon:** Clean single-pass arcs.
   * **Elemental Dagger:** Straight blade line + crossguard symmetry.

---

## 🔍 2. The Reverse Side Test (Depth Calibration)

Turn your 3 mm silicone practice skin upside down:
* ❌ **Sliced like a scalpel / cut through:** You are pressing too deep (> 2.0 mm). Back off the hand pressure and reduce needle hang to 1.2 mm.
* ❌ **Faint grey or no indentation:** You are skimming too shallow (< 1.0 mm) or moving too fast.
* ✅ **Gentle tactile embossing without tearing:** **PERFECT DERMAL DEPTH (1.5 mm).** You are ready to tattoo Marceline and Hello Kitty!
