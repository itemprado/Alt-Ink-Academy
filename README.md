## 🦾 Hardware Profile

The curriculum is calibrated specifically for rotary pen tattoo machines:

| Component | Specification | Operational Protocol |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Machine** | **Poseidon Pen HM142-2** | 3.5 mm fixed stroke, coreless motor (~8,000 RPM at 7.2V). |
| **Power Supply** | **Wireless Digital LED Battery** | 1200 mAh USB-C, 0.1V step calibration (Optimal: 5.8V – 7.4V). |
| **Linework Cartridge** | **1205RL (5 Round Liner)** | 0.35mm gauge, tight grouping, long taper. Linework hang: **1.5 mm**. |
| **Shading Cartridge** | **1207RM (7 Curved Magnum)** | 0.35mm gauge, arched soft edge. Shading hang: **1.8 mm**. |
| **Practice Substrate** | **Silicone Synthetic Skin** | 3 mm thickness, fixed to table with painter's tape on 4 corners. |

## 🛡️ The Golden Workshop Protocols

### 1. The Ink Dipping Protocol
1. **Always dip with machine ON at 7.2V:** 5 needle tips oscillating at 70 Hz generate a capillary Venturi vacuum. Dipping with power off will not load ink into the reservoir.
2. **1 to 2 mm tip depth only:** Never submerge the cartridge barrel or ink will seep into the motor shaft.
3. **1 to 2 seconds:** Count *"one-one-thousand, two-one-thousand"*, then withdraw.
4. **The Cup Rim Tap (CRITICAL):** Touch the plastic needle nozzle against the inner lip of the ink cap upon exit. This breaks the hanging ink drop. *Skipping this step will flood your stencil with an uncontrollable ink puddle!*

### 2. The Vaseline Shield
* Synthetic silicone skin is microporous. Wiping dry paper across fresh ink forces carbon pigment into surface pores, leaving a dark grey stain that destroys the stencil.
* **The Solution:** Apply an ultra-thin, almost invisible film of pure petroleum jelly (Vaseline) over the skin **BEFORE** laying down your first line. The needle penetrates 1.5 mm into the silicone, while surface excess floats harmlessly on the hydrophobic lipid layer.

### 3. Dab & Lift Cleaning Technique
* **During Tattooing:** Fold kitchen paper towels, moisten lightly with **clean water** (never alcohol). Gently press down over the line (*dab*) to absorb excess ink, then lift vertically. **Never rub or drag dry paper across the transfer!**
* **After Finishing:** Soak a towel with **70% isopropyl alcohol** and vigorously buff the synthetic skin to dissolve leftover vaseline and purge purple stencil ink, revealing crisp black lines on a stark white background.
