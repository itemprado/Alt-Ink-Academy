## ⚡ 1. The 10-Point Preflight Checklist

Before powering on your **Poseidon Pen**, verify each item:
- [ ] **1. Poseidon Pen 3.5mm:** Battery at 100%, pre-tested at 7.2V.
- [ ] **2. Cartridge Insertion:** `1205RL` (linework) or `1207RM` (shading) locked with a 90° clockwise click.
- [ ] **3. Pear-Shaped Grip Wrap:** 2 folds of soft paper core + cohesive grip tape (35–40 mm thickness), leaving 3 mm clearance below the adjustment collar.
- [ ] **4. Ink Cap at 3/4:** Anchored with petroleum jelly, filled to 3/4 with pure black tattoo ink.
- [ ] **5. Vaseline Barrier:** Pure petroleum jelly dollop transferred via clean wooden tongue depressor.
- [ ] **6. Moistened Dab Paper:** Kitchen paper towels folded and moistened with clean water (no alcohol).
- [ ] **7. Needle Rinse Cup:** Clean distilled water cup for nozzle flushes.
- [ ] **8. Synthetic Skin Secured:** 3 mm silicone sheet taped firmly at all 4 corners with masking tape.
- [ ] **9. Nitrile Gloves:** Snug fit on both hands (sanitation + pinky skate friction reduction).
- [ ] **10. Cured Transfer:** Stencil ink cured for 8 to 12 hours prior to session.

## 🛡️ 3. The 4 Golden Biomechanical Rules

1. **The 75° Golden Drag Angle:**  
   Always drag the Poseidon Pen tilted at **75° away from the direction of travel**. Ink flows down via capillary action. Tatting at 90° bounces and spatters; tatting at <60° causes ink to spread sideways (subcutaneous blowout).
2. **The 45° Skimming Whip (1207RM):**  
   For velvet shadows and corset folds, skim the curved magnum at **45°**. The 7 staggered needles comb the skin like watercolor bristles.
3. **Rotate the Canvas, Never Strain Your Wrist:**  
   When tattooing curves, **rotate the silicone sheet with your free hand**. Your machine hand should always pull toward your chest in its strongest, most stable ergonomic arc.
4. **The Cup Rim Tap:**  
   After dipping into ink for 1-2 seconds with the motor on, lightly tap the nozzle against the cap rim to shed the hanging droplet.
